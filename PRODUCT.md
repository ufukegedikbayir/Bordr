# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Open decision. Candidates are customs brokers, in-house trade-compliance teams, and freight forwarders. The product is B2B: users sign in to do operational customs work, not to browse.

## Product Purpose

Bordr is a B2B SaaS for cross-border customs work. Its core promise is automated filing: turning trade documents (commercial invoices, packing lists) into ready-to-file customs declarations with far less manual entry.

## Positioning

Automated filing — less manual data entry between source documents and a filed declaration. Specific mechanism and supported customs regimes are not yet defined.

## Operating Context

Professional, desk-based work in a browser. Accounts are email/password via Supabase Auth with email confirmation required.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Next.js 16 App Router, TypeScript (strict), Tailwind CSS v4, Supabase (auth + Postgres).
- Built in phases; marketing pages are out of scope until a later phase.
- Undecided: target markets/customs regimes, pricing, roles.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Bordr. No logo, palette, or brand assets exist yet. The owner asked for a minimalist, strictly professional, "Vercel/Linear tier" feel.

## Evidence on Hand

None. Do not fabricate customers, testimonials, certifications, compliance badges, or metrics.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before flourish — this handles regulated filings; every surface should read as precise and dependable.
2. Operators' time is the product — fewer steps and less typing beat decoration.
3. Never overclaim — no invented proof, regimes, or guarantees.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is required (enterprise buyers).
